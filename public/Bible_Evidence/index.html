OBSERVE · CONNECT · EXPLORE

A passage opens a point.
A point opens a world.

The books provide the path. Each section marks its narrative or historical setting; each discovery opens into a passage, an observation, and the sources behind their connection.

How do we help you explore?

We bring discoveries, surviving texts, and their biblical connections into one accessible reference. Explanations help you read unfamiliar names, customs, and dates. Open the sources, compare the material, and reach your own conclusions.

What goes into a data point?

Every recovered text is a data point, including fragments and repeated copies. Named kings preserved in historical records and places and geographic names indexed in the Bible also have entries. A person or place can link to several discoveries, and one inscription can name several people. Follow those connections within the same book-by-book timeline.

What do the quotations show?

Each tile quotes a selected Bible passage in the King James Version. Excerpts are labeled and the full selected verse is available on the detail page. Where the index records a name in another translation, the passage notes explain the difference. Each geographic entry retains every indexed verse reference. The quoted Bible passage sits beside the discovery’s own observation and sources. Selected ancient names and short translated excerpts appear with their edition references; explanatory summaries are presented as prose.

Where do the sources come from?

The collection draws on museum and library catalogs, excavation publications, scholarly editions, and research. Geographic names and verse connections incorporate OpenBible.info’s Bible Geocoding Data, adapted under CC BY 4.0. Open a source to see what it supports and how it was accessed. The source library also lists major collections for further research.

How does the collection grow?

The timeline includes all 1,285 verse-linked entries from the recorded OpenBible geographic index, alongside the growing collection of artifacts, people, and surviving texts. New observations become points with their own dates and sources. The downloadable dataset preserves every point and its connections so the collection can be reused and extended.
