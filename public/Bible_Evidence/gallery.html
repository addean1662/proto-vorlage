THE KETEF HINNOM AMULETS

LOOK CLOSER · NUMBERS 6

A familiar blessing.
An extraordinary survival.

Two tiny silver amulets, discovered in a Jerusalem burial cave in 1979, carry language also found in the priestly blessing of Numbers.

“The LORD bless thee,
and keep thee”Numbers 6:24 · King James Version

These objects preserve an early form of a biblical blessing. Their connection to the text is remarkable; the date of the whole book of Numbers is a separate question.

A few words you’ll encounter

What is a textual witness?

A surviving copy, quotation, or parallel wording that helps scholars study a text’s history. A witness can preserve both continuity and variation.

What is a stele?

An upright stone monument, often bearing an inscription or image. Royal stelae commonly commemorate victories or public acts.

How is an artifact dated?

Methods include excavation context, comparisons of letter forms and object styles, dated historical references, and scientific analysis where suitable. The most useful dates draw on several kinds of evidence.
